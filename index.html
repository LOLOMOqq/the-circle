About this journal

STM213 is dedicated to John Wendell Calzita. It is a running timeline of his circle — birthdays, reunions, road trips, graduation walks, quiet Sundays — written by the friends who shared them with him.

The house rule: every post with a picture must name the people and the place in the picture. That way, years from now, the search will still find them all.

Search by school: the timeline can be filtered by the two schools that shaped it. Click one and the friends of that school appear above the posts.
